# A1 – Create Portfolio 

## Objective
To Build a Professional Portfolio

## Analyze
Task A:

Portfolio #1 (https://nhoong.github.io/index.html):  It is a relatively simple website design which makes it very navigable for anyone who may come across it. It features two separate tabs, each featuring a scrollable design with consistent section headings that make it easy to skim. The reproducibility is limited with how he describes his projects, everything is about the justification and the result, very little to do with actual procedure. Evidence of reasoning is also weak considering there are no mentions of any governing models, trade-offs, or decision criteria. The language of the portfolio is professional and uses clear and concise phrasing.

Portfolio #2 (https://sites.google.com/view/sethschafferportfolio/combat-robots):  The website struggles with navigability because there are many different separate areas that are very dense and not distinctly labeled, leading to an overcrowded layout, requiring more time to find whatever someone may be looking for. The portfolio reads like a story, which lends itself to him explaining the decision making and lessons learned, but it hurts the reproducibility of his projects, as there is not much information on the procedure involved. He does an excellent job of showing the iteration, via a detailed timeline with pictures and dates, and reasoning behind all versions of his projects listed as near every failure is mentioned with a point of failure and a way that it was improved. The tone of the portfolio leans a bit casual, with small bits of comedy here and there, instead of strictly professional documentation.
