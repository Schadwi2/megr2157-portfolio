#About Me
## Sean Chadwick

Ever since I was young, I was always interested in how things worked mechanically, whether it was an engine or a simple pen. What specifically drew me to mechanical engineering was a passion for small engines and the desire to learn how to design them. In high school, I worked as a small engine repairman, which gave me a familiarity with mechanical systems and a systematic way of thinking. along the way I had many personal projects, professional conversations, and opportunities that allowed me to find out my engineering passion actually lies with HVAC systems. This switch was inspired by three major factors. firstly, I had worked installing HVAC systems for a brief time when I was in high school, so I had an interest in learning more about the design. Secondly, I enjoyed my Thermodynamics-based course work a lot more than my work centered around part design. lastly, a conversation with an engineer at a career fair gave me the quote, "some engineers design the individual part, and others will design the whole system, you just have to choose which one you would like to handle," which inspired the switch to my current industry. I currently work as a mechanical design engineer, which consists of designing mechanical systems in large industrial buildings, such as data centers and advanced manufacturing facilities. The complexity of the work and the amount of communication that happens between separate teams within the industry has made for an excellent learning experience while I am in college, and I am excited every day to learn more.
